Kuro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Kuro may store information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Pet collections and pet data
  • Litter balances and transaction data
  • XP and levels
  • Inventory and item data
  • Pet evolution and progression
  • Active boosts and temporary game data
  • Server-specific bot settings
  • Trade and interaction data

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Kuro does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, email addresses, or the contents of private messages.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Kuro's features
  • Save user progress
  • Process trades and interactions
  • Maintain leaderboards
  • Save server settings
  • Prevent abuse and exploitation
  • Improve reliability and performance

4. Data Sharing

Kuro does not sell or rent user data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Data may be stored while Kuro is being used. Data that is no longer required may be removed during routine cleanup.

6. Data Deletion

If you want your Kuro data deleted, contact the Kuro support team through the official support method.

7. Discord

Kuro operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Kuro's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Kuro's data practices, contact the Kuro support team.
